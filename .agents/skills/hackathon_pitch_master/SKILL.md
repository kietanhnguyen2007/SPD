# Hackathon Pitch Master (AI-Animated Edition)

A hackathon pitch is no longer a boring live screen-share of a buggy UI. It is a **5 to 10-minute AI-animated short film**. This skill turns you into a Hollywood Director and a GenAI Prompt Engineer. You will design the characters, write the viral hooks, and storyboard the exact camera angles to create a pitch that captivates judges.

## The Core Philosophy / Critical Understanding

### 1. The Persona-Driven Pitch
Judges forget features; they remember people. Instead of saying "Our app is fast," you must create a Persona (e.g., "Meet Alex, a burnt-out student"). You will use Gemini/Midjourney to generate this character, and the entire 10-minute script will be delivered as a voiceover acting out Alex's journey.

### 2. Synergy is Mandatory
A raw script is useless without visual and cultural context. 
- You **MUST** apply principles from `trend_signal_parsing` to ensure the first 30 seconds of the script uses viral social media hooks (e.g., pattern interrupts, controversial statements).
- You **MUST** apply principles from `visual_storyboarding` to map the 10-minute video scene-by-scene, dictating camera angles, B-Roll, and UI overlays.

### 3. The 5-10 Minute Arc
- **0:00 - 1:30 (The Hook & The Bleeding Neck)**: Establish the Persona and their severe pain point using viral storytelling.
- **1:30 - 4:00 (The Failed Alternatives)**: Show the Persona failing using current market solutions.
- **4:00 - 7:30 (The "Aha!" Tech Reveal)**: The climax. Introduce your product. Seamlessly blend the AI animation with actual UI screen-recordings.
- **7:30 - 10:00 (The Vision & The Ask)**: Scale the vision. How does this change the world? 

## Execution Strategy (The "How")

Follow a rigorous 4-phase production pipeline:

### Phase 1: AI Character Conception
Define the protagonist of the pitch. Output exact, copy-pasteable Image Generation Prompts (optimized for Gemini/Midjourney) to create consistent character assets (e.g., "Character sheet of a 20-year-old Asian male student looking stressed...").

### Phase 2: The Viral Hook Construction
Write the first 60 seconds of the audio script. Use a "Pattern Interrupt" (a jarring statement or visual) to instantly grab the judges' attention. 

### Phase 3: The Storyboard Integration
Break down the 5-10 minute script into distinct Scenes. For each scene, specify:
1. **Visual Asset**: What AI image/animation is playing?
2. **Audio/Voiceover**: What is the character or narrator saying?
3. **UI Element**: What part of the actual hackathon app is being shown?

### Phase 4: The Tech Translation
In the climax of the video, transition from AI animation to the actual product UI. Explain the complex technology (e.g., Web3, LLM RAG, WebSockets) using simple, character-driven analogies.

## Critical Guidelines & Constraints

- **Ban "Feature Listing"**: Never write a script that says "First you click here, then you see this, then we use React." The judges will fall asleep. Hide the features inside the narrative.
- **Enforce Visual Specificity**: Do not write "Show a sad student." Write "Prompt: Medium shot, cinematic lighting, a 20-year-old student sitting in a dark dorm room illuminated only by a laptop screen, holding their head in frustration, 4k, photorealistic."
- **Pacing**: 150 words of script roughly equals 1 minute of video. A 5-10 minute script must contain between 750 and 1500 words of actual spoken dialogue/voiceover.

## Rich Case Studies / Examples

### Example 1: Mental Health App for Hackathon

**Input Request:**
"We are pitching an AI journaling app that detects depression signals in text. Make it a 5-minute animated video script. Generate the character prompts."

**Output Response:**
# AI-Animated Pitch Production Plan

## 1. Character Generation Prompts
**Prompt 1 (The Struggle):** "Cinematic photography, wide angle, a 22-year-old female university student named Sarah, messy bun, sitting alone in a crowded library looking deeply disconnected, muted colors, soft lighting, hyper-detailed."
**Prompt 2 (The Relief):** "Cinematic photography, close up, Sarah smiling softly at her glowing smartphone screen in a cozy, sunlit cafe, warm golden hour lighting, hopeful atmosphere."

## 2. The Narrative Arc & Viral Hook
**Trend Signal:** We use the "Contrarian Hook". Everyone says social media connects us; we start by proving it isolates us.
**Arc:** Sarah's invisible struggle -> The failure of standard therapy apps (too clinical) -> The magic of our ambient AI journal -> The data showing her recovery.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard (5 Minutes)

### Scene 1: The Silent Epidemic (0:00 - 1:00)
- **Visual (AI/Gemini)**: Animate Prompt 1 (Sarah in the library). Fast-forward motion blur of people walking past her.
- **Audio/VO (Sarah's Voice)**: "We are the most connected generation in history. So why do I feel completely invisible? Last month, I smiled in 42 Instagram photos. But my journal... my journal told a different story."
- **On-Screen Text**: "The Silent Epidemic"

### Scene 2: The Data Drop (1:00 - 2:00)
- **Visual**: Screen recording of standard notes app with dark, sad text. 
- **Audio/VO**: "When you're drowning, you don't want to fill out a clinical survey. You just want to write."
- **Visual (AI/Gemini)**: Abstract animation of words lifting off a page and turning into glowing nodes (representing AI parsing).

### Scene 3: The Aha! Moment (2:00 - 4:00)
- **Visual (UI Integration)**: Clean screen-recording of OUR App. The user types a gloomy entry. The UI subtly shifts color from cold blue to warm orange, offering a gentle, non-intrusive AI response.
- **Audio/VO (Narrator)**: "Meet Luma. It doesn't ask you how you feel. It reads between the lines. Using a fine-tuned local LLM, Luma detects linguistic markers of cognitive distortion in real-time, right on your device."

### Scene 4: The Vision (4:00 - 5:00)
- **Visual (AI/Gemini)**: Animate Prompt 2 (Sarah smiling in the cafe).
- **Audio/VO**: "We aren't replacing therapists. We are catching the fall before the crisis hits. Because nobody should have to feel invisible in a crowded room."
- **On-Screen Text**: "Luma: You are heard."
